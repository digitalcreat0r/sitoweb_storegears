# Privacy Policy - PigeonTweak

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

**PigeonTweak** is an application for Shopify merchants, developed and managed by **{{ site.author }}** ("the developer"), operating as **{{ site.brand }}**. It lets a signed-in customer correct the shipping address on an order from the order status page, while the merchant's edit window is still open and the order can still be changed. The country on the address stays the same. This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, and how it is handled.

## 1. Data Controller

Email: {{ site.email }}<br>

For store data and for customer data that PigeonTweak reads or updates in your Shopify store (including a customer's name, shipping address, and phone number on an order), you (the merchant) are the data controller. The developer acts as a data processor and handles that data only to provide the service you have installed.

## 2. Information Collection
PigeonTweak processes personal information about your customers only so a customer who owns an order can correct that order's shipping address, and so you can be told that the address changed. It does not build a customer list, and it does not read customer email addresses from Shopify.

### Store and order data accessed through Shopify
When you install the app, you grant it the following access scopes:

- **`read_orders`:** Used to read the order the customer is viewing: order name and id, whether it is cancelled or closed, when it was created, the current shipping address (name, company, street, city, province, postal code, country, and phone), fulfillment state, and the order total. This is how the app decides whether a correction is still allowed and what address to show.
- **`write_orders`:** Used only to save the shipping address the customer submits back onto that order. The app does not change line items, discounts, or products. Shopify may recalculate shipping after the address changes; the app reads the order total before and after so it can tell you if the total changed.
- **`read_customers`:** Used so the customer session token identifies the signed-in customer, and so the app can confirm that this customer owns the order. A guest order with no customer account cannot be changed here. The app does not read a customer's email address, phone number from the customer profile, or marketing preferences.
- **`read_merchant_managed_fulfillment_orders`:** Used to read whether a fulfillment request is already submitted or accepted. Once it is, the address can no longer be changed.
- **`write_app_proxy`:** Used so the order status form can call the app through your shop domain.

Name, shipping address, and phone are protected customer data. The app requests them only for this correction flow.

The app also reads basic store details: your shop name, shop domain, store email address, and time zone. The store email is the address where merchant notices are sent. It is read from Shopify when a notice is sent and is not copied into the app's settings. The time zone is used to apply your quiet hours.

### Data the customer provides
On the order status page, the signed-in customer may submit a new shipping address. The fields you leave enabled can include first name, last name, company, street address, city, province or state, postal code, and phone. The country cannot be changed. The app checks that the customer owns the order, that your edit window is still open, that nothing has been fulfilled, and that no fulfillment request has been submitted or accepted. It then writes the address to the order in Shopify.

### Data you provide in the app
- **Settings:** Whether corrections are enabled, the edit window (from 1 hour to 30 days), which address fields the form shows, whether to email you when an address changes, whether to email you only when the order total also changes, and optional quiet hours.

### Data generated by the app
- **Correction log:** For each attempt, the app stores your shop domain, the Shopify order id, the outcome (for example, updated or not allowed), and the time. This log does not store the customer's name, address, or phone. It is used to show you how many corrections succeeded. Entries older than 90 days are deleted.
- **Held merchant notice:** If you turn on quiet hours, a notice that falls inside those hours is stored until the quiet period ends. That stored notice contains the order name, the Shopify order id, the previous and new shipping address (including name and phone), and the order total before and after the change. It is deleted after the email is sent.
- **Technical records:** Authentication sessions for your shop (shop domain, access token, granted scopes, and, for an online staff session, the staff member's Shopify user id, name, and email) and a count of test emails sent today, so the app can run and can limit test sends to three per day.

### Support and feedback (voluntary)
If you contact the developer by email, you may voluntarily provide your name, email address, and your shop domain. This information is used solely to respond to your request and resolve technical issues. It is not used for any other purpose.

### Analytics, advertising, and tracking
PigeonTweak does **not** use third-party analytics tools, advertising trackers, or profiling. It does not sell or share your data, or your customers' data, for marketing purposes. The correction count in the app admin is calculated from the correction log described above.

## 3. How a correction is saved and how you are notified
The new address is written to the order in your Shopify admin. It is not kept as a separate address book in the app.

If merchant notices are on, the app emails your store email address. The message names the order and includes the previous address and the new address. If the order total changed and you have asked to be told about that, the message also includes the previous total and the new total, and a link to the order in Shopify admin. A test email uses sample data only and does not read an order.

- **Email:** Sent through **Resend** (resend.com). Your store email address and the notice (including the customer name, shipping address, and phone number on that order) are processed by Resend only to deliver the message. See the [Resend Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Quiet hours:** A notice that falls in the hours you choose is held in the app database and sent when that window ends. Times follow your store's time zone.

## 4. Third-party services
- **Shopify:** PigeonTweak runs inside the Shopify admin and on the order status page, and it uses the Shopify Admin API and customer account session tokens. Shopify processes data according to its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy). The shipping address is stored on the order in Shopify, under Shopify's terms and your own store policies.
- **Resend:** Merchant notice delivery, as described above.
- **Hosting:** The app is hosted on Fly.io. Its database is a SQLite file on an encrypted volume in the app's primary region (Chicago, United States). The host stores data on the developer's behalf to run the service.

## 5. Data storage, security, and retention
App data is stored in a SQLite database on an encrypted volume. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access is limited to the developer.

- **Active use:** Shop settings, sessions, and the correction log are kept while the app is installed, so corrections and the correction count keep working. Correction log entries are deleted after 90 days. A held merchant notice is kept only until the email is sent, then deleted.
- **Uninstall:** When you uninstall PigeonTweak, the app receives Shopify's `app/uninstalled` notification and deletes the data it holds for your store: sessions, shop settings, the correction log, and any merchant notice still held for quiet hours.
- **Shopify compliance requests:** The app handles Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks. `shop/redact` is sent about 48 hours after uninstall and deletes any store data still held. `customers/redact` deletes the correction log and any held notice for the orders named in the request. The app does not keep a customer profile. On `customers/data_request`, if a correction log entry or a held notice matches the orders in the request, that data is emailed to the store email address. If nothing is held, there is no customer data to return.
- **What is not copied out of Shopify:** The app does not keep a lasting copy of the shipping address. After a notice is sent, or after uninstall, the address remains on the order in Shopify, which you control. The correction log keeps the order id and the time, not the address.
- **Support emails:** Kept only as long as needed to handle your request, and then deleted unless a longer period is required by law.

## 6. Security incidents
The developer is responsible for containing a security incident that exposes customer name, shipping address, phone number, or order data processed by the app. If the developer becomes aware of such an incident, the developer contains it and notifies Shopify at [security@shopify.com](mailto:security@shopify.com) within 24 hours. Affected merchants are notified as required by applicable law.

## 7. International data transfers
The developer and the service providers listed above may process data in countries outside your own, including in the United States and outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are applied as required by applicable law.

## 8. Compliance and rights
In accordance with the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable privacy laws, data subjects have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export their data, and to object to its processing. Those rights are exercised as follows:

- **Your store data:** Change or clear settings in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to delete the sessions, settings, correction log, and any held notice described in Section 5. Contact the developer for other requests.
- **Your customer's data:** You are the data controller for customer order data. A customer should contact your store about an order. If a request requires something the app still holds (a held notice, or an order id in the correction log), Shopify sends `customers/data_request` or `customers/redact`, and you can also contact the developer at the email address below with the shop domain. Requests are answered within 30 days.

PigeonTweak does not sell or share personal information, and it does not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 9. Children's privacy
PigeonTweak is a business tool for Shopify merchants and is not directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16 years of age beyond the shipping details a customer submits on an order the merchant already holds.
