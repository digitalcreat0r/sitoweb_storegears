# Cookie Policy - {{ site.brand }}

*Last updated: May 21, 2026*

This Cookie Policy explains how **{{ site.brand }}**, developed and managed by **{{ site.author }}** ("the developer"), uses cookies and similar technologies, such as local storage, on this website. Your privacy is respected, and the developer aims to be fully transparent about the data stored on your device.

## 1. What are Cookies and Local Storage?
Cookies are small text files placed on your device to help a website provide a better user experience. Local storage is a standard web technology that allows a website to store information locally within your browser.

## 2. How the Developer Uses These Technologies
This website is built with privacy in mind. The developer does not use intrusive tracking cookies, third-party advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that monitor your browsing behavior across other sites.

The only storage technologies currently utilized are strictly necessary for the website's core functionality and user experience:
- **Theme Preference (Local Storage):** The developer uses your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) to remember your display preference (Light Mode or Dark Mode) across sessions. This data is not transmitted to any server and remains solely on your device.
- **Session and Security Cookies:** If you interact with contact forms, minimal session cookies may be generated to prevent spam, ensure security, and maintain a secure session.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies
The developer currently does not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools on this website. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and your explicit consent will be requested, as required by law, before placing any non-essential cookies on your device.

## 4. Managing Your Preferences
You can choose to disable or clear your browser's local storage and cookies at any time through your web browser's settings. Please note that clearing your local storage will simply reset your Light/Dark mode preference to the default setting.
